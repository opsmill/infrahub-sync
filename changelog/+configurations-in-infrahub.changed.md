Configurations now live in Infrahub as `SyncConfiguration` nodes, from the `schema/sync.yml` extension, and each run records the version it uses; `configs register` and `configs version` are refused with `410 configurations-in-infrahub`, and `diff` and `sync` no longer need `--version`. Callers authenticate with their own Infrahub API token (`INFRAHUB_SYNC_TOKEN`), Sync checks their Infrahub permissions, and `INFRAHUB_SYNC_SERVICE_BEARER_TOKENS` is replaced by the service account settings `INFRAHUB_SYNC_INFRAHUB_ADDRESS` and `INFRAHUB_SYNC_INFRAHUB_TOKEN`.
