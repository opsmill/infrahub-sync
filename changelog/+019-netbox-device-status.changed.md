The NetBox to Infrahub example (`examples/netbox_to_infrahub/`, in `package.yml` and `config.yml`) now maps each NetBox device status to a choice of the schema library's `DcimDevice.status`: `planned` and `staged` to `provisioning`, `offline` and `failed` to `maintenance`, `inventory` to `reserved`, and `decommissioning` to `deprecated`; `active` is kept. A status missing from the mapping is kept as it is, and the plan leaves that device out and lists it as a `dropdown_value_not_in_choices` problem, instead of the apply stopping when Infrahub rejects the value. The example's README lists each pair.
