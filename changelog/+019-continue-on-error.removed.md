The `continue_on_error` parameter is removed from `get_potenda_from_instance`, `execute_run`, the `PotendaFactory` protocol and `Potenda`, and Potenda no longer sets a `continue_on_error` attribute on the adapters. A plan now records each source record it cannot build, leaves it out and lists it as a problem, so the option to skip such records has no use. A caller that passes `continue_on_error` gets a `TypeError`; remove the argument.
