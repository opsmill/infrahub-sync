A plan no longer stops at the first source record it cannot use. It records every problem, leaves out the records that have one and the records that depend on them through a required relationship, keeps records that refer to them through an optional relationship without that relationship, and plans the rest. Two source records that Infrahub would store as one node are left out with one problem, instead of refusing the plan. Plans are saved in format 4, which adds `plan/report.jsonl`.
