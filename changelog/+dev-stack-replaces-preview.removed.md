The preview stack is removed: `invoke preview.*`, `development/docker-compose.preview.yml` and `development/preview.env` are gone, and the development stack (`invoke dev.start`) replaces them. The smoke suite moves to `tests/dev_stack/smoke/` under the `smoke` marker and runs with `invoke dev.smoke`. `format` and `lint` are now the only top-level Invoke tasks: use `docs.docusaurus`, `docs.generate`, `tests.tests-unit`, `tests.tests-integration`, `tests.all`, `tests.check-310`, `dev.build`, `dev.start` and `dev.destroy` instead of the removed top-level names, and `netbox.start` and `netbox.destroy` instead of `netbox.up` and `netbox.down`.
