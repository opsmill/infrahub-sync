The responses of `configs register` and `configs version` (`POST /configs` and `POST /configs/{config_id}/versions`) no longer include `declared_content`. They return the `config_id`, `registry_version`, `package_checksum` and `created_at` of the registered version. Read the content with `configs show <config-id> --version <version>` or `GET /configs/{config_id}/versions/{registry_version}`.
