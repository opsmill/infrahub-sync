Added `GET /runs/{run_id}/logs` and the `infrahub-sync runs logs` command. They print the lines that Sync wrote during a run, with their stage, level and time, without access to Prefect. `--stage` limits the lines to one stage and `--follow` prints new lines until the run ends. A log that Prefect no longer holds returns `log-expired`; when Prefect cannot be reached, the request returns `log-unavailable`.
