+Added a Webex source adapter with configurable read-only resources, token renewal, pagination and an example mapping locations, workspaces and devices with peer relationships. Default retry backoff is capped by the `max_retry_delay` setting, a `Retry-After` longer than `max_retry_delay` stops the run, and `{env: ...}` references are accepted in `org_id` and `params` only; the `*_env` credential settings take environment variable names.
