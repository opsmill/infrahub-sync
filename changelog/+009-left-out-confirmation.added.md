Applying a plan that leaves out records now requires the number of left-out records: `infrahub-sync apply --confirm-left-out <count>` in the CLI, or `confirmed_left_out_count` in `POST /runs/{run_id}/apply`. Without the count, or with a different count, the apply is refused with 409 `left-out-confirmation-required` before any write. A plan saved in format 2 or 3 is refused with 409 `plan-format-not-applicable` and must be planned again. A `sync` run whose plan leaves out records stops after the plan with outcome `left-out-review-required`, and is then applied with the confirmed count. `infrahub-sync runs show` prints the operations written and the left-out count of a completed apply.
