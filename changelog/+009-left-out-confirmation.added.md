Applying a plan that leaves out records now requires a confirmation that the records left out were reviewed: `infrahub-sync apply --confirm-left-out` in the CLI, or `confirm_left_out: true` in `POST /runs/{run_id}/apply`. Without it, the apply is refused with 409 `left-out-confirmation-required` before any write, and the refusal states how many records the plan leaves out. A plan saved in format 2 or 3 is refused with 409 `plan-format-not-applicable` and must be planned again. A `sync` run whose plan leaves out records stops after the plan with outcome `left-out-review-required`, and is then applied with the confirmation. `infrahub-sync runs show` prints the operations written and the left-out count of a completed apply.
