A second diff after syncing link aggregation group (LAG) members now completes instead of failing when a LAG peer lacks its device identifier.
A relationship peer missing from the DiffSync store because its kind is outside `order`, it is excluded by `filter_records`, re-keyed by `transform_records`, or skipped under `--continue-on-error` previously stopped the load with `ObjectNotFound`; now the load continues and the parent keeps that peer's identity without adding the peer as a sync record.
A missing or null peer identifier now raises `PeerIdentifierError`, and `--continue-on-error` skips that relationship.
A peer with a cardinality-many relationship identifier now raises `PeerSdkAliasError` where it previously returned an ID; shipped examples do not use such an identifier.
