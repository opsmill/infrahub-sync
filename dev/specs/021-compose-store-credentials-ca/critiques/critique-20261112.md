# Critique Report: an external object store, every adapter's credentials and a private CA in the Compose deployment

**Date**: 2026-11-12
**Spec**: [spec.md](../spec.md) at `523c7239`
**Plan**: [plan.md](../plan.md) at `4eed1d85`

## Executive Summary

The problem is sourced: specification 010 defers these items to a second feature, and Jira card
INFP-692 lists them as blockers. The scope is the remainder of old 013 after 010 and 007. Two
findings must be fixed before tasks: the release notes job cannot get the write permission the plan
gives it, and one combination of object store settings fails with a misleading failure name. Both
are small. Verdict: proceed with updates.

### Operational Readiness

| ID | Severity | Finding | Suggestion |
|----|----------|---------|------------|
| E7 | 🎯 | The `record_image` job needs `contents: write` to edit the GitHub Release. `workflow-publish.yml` is called by `trigger-release.yml`, whose `publish` job grants `contents: read` and `id-token: write` (`trigger-release.yml:17-21`). A called workflow can only keep or narrow the caller's token, so raising `workflow-publish.yml:60` alone has no effect. | Raise the caller's `publish` job to `contents: write`, keep the top-level permission of `workflow-publish.yml` at `contents: read`, and give `contents: write` only to the `record_image` job. Add a workflow contract test that checks both. |
