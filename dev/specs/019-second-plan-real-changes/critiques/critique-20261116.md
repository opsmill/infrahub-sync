# Critique Report: A second plan shows only real changes, and the plan step is measured by part

**Date**: 2026-11-16

**Artifacts reviewed**: `spec.md` (`cbacabd3`), `plan.md`, `research.md`, `data-model.md`,
`contracts/`, `quickstart.md` (`5ce138d4`), `.specify/memory/constitution.md`.

## Executive Summary

The spec and the plan target a real, recorded defect: a second plan is never empty and misses
one kind of change (#387). The design reuses existing patterns: the read observer of ADR 0035,
the start-up column migration, and the meaning of an empty peer list for cardinality many.

Four findings must be addressed before tasks:

- the spec asks for a stop time that the design cannot always give;
- the spec says "record" for parts that the design derives;
- the rule for nodes that Infrahub creates itself may cover IP namespaces only;
- the apply change could alter writes that hold no emptied relationship.

All four are resolvable by editing the spec and the plan.

**Verdict**: ⚠️ PROCEED WITH UPDATES.
